# Surface: Proposal Presentation

Mode: Persuade. The instructor and classmates decide whether the proposal is credible; the deck is the product.

## Audience and job

CSC 172 instructor and classmates, with the presenter speaking live. They need to understand the problem, the six-stage loop, the shared CV/DM evidence, and the limits of the claim quickly enough to judge the proposal.

## Proof and content

ScreenSpot-Pro: 1,581 total examples, 1,020 Windows examples, 23 professional applications, 3 operating systems. CART as the single primary algorithm; association rules, random forest, logistic regression, and clustering as supporting analyses. JSONL → DuckDB → Parquet, with Arrow as an in-memory handoff. MIT dataset license, with screenshot rights remaining with application vendors. No implementation results.

## Constraints

11 slides; static HTML/CSS/JS; GitHub Pages; keyboard and swipe navigation; 1280×720 or larger; proposal-only; no fabricated results or measured performance. Motion is authored and intentionally not reduced: the CRT loops, signal beams, status lights, boot replay, and CART trace run on this presentation surface.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dark terminal-style proposal deck for Glasshand. CRT texture and system-check motion are the presentation surface, not a general-purpose website.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black phosphor ground; green and amber status; scanlines, ghost GLASSHAND, mono data, Saira Condensed display, square panels, system-check rhythm, a signal beam crossing the pipeline, and diagrams for the stage loop, dataset record format, evidence chains, analysis order, scope strip, and artifact chain.

STORY: The visitor watches a proposal run as a system: problem, six-stage loop, contributions, dataset, questions, method, CART, scope. They can jump by segment, select a pipeline stage, replay the system check, and trace the CART failure path. The problem, dataset, questions, and supporting-analysis slides each carry a diagram that shows the relationship the rows describe; Contributions adds one flow and one output per contribution. The cover system check auto-replays 350 ms after first paint, while the CRT power-on settles, and restarts 40 ms after returning to the cover; its terminal prompt is optional.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed CRT screen. On desktop, a six-line headline dominates the left; below 900px it wraps naturally as sentences. A system-check panel on the right shows the proposal's stage status; the top and bottom chrome carry the slide count and proposal state. The headline is the thesis, not a header.
